AntCloud Native is a personal macOS client based on Moonlight PC 6.2.0. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by AntCloud or Moonlight.

Moonlight's original GPL-3.0 license, copyright notices and dependency licenses are retained under src/. New AntCloud account/session/UI/input modifications are provided under GPL-3.0-or-later; the combined distribution must satisfy the upstream GPL-3.0 requirements.

The source package includes the complete modified engine and public submodule sources. The Mac SDK is downloaded separately from the pinned official Moonlight dependency release and verified against its recorded SHA-256. Retain the SDK/dependency license notices in any binary distribution.

No private HAR captures, provider installers, account credentials, saved tokens or live diagnostic logs are included.
